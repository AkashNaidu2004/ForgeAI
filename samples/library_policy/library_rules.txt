LIBRARY BORROWING POLICY

Students may borrow up to 4 books at a time.

Faculty members may borrow up to 8 books at a time.

The standard student borrowing period is 14 days.

The faculty borrowing period is 30 days.

Overdue books incur a fine of INR 5 per book per day.

Books may be renewed once for an additional 7 days if no reservation exists.

Lost books must be replaced or paid for at their replacement cost.

Reference books cannot be borrowed.

The library operates Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM.

Membership and borrowing disputes must be referred to the librarian.
